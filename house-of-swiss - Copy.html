Our Maison

More than time —
an inheritance
of obsession.

Founded in Geneva in 1887, House of Swiss began as a single atelier where master watchmaker Henri Beaumont spent fifteen years perfecting a single escapement. One hundred and thirty-seven years later, we remain guided by that same singular devotion: the relentless pursuit of mechanical perfection, one heartbeat at a time.

Every timepiece leaves our maison bearing the Geneva Seal — a promise written in steel, sapphire and precious metal that cannot be faked, only earned.

Atelier

The Art of Precision

01
In-House Movements

Every calibre is designed, engineered and assembled within our Geneva atelier. From the hairspring to the escape wheel — no detail is delegated, no compromise accepted.

02
Geneva Seal Certified

The Poinçon de Genève is the most demanding hallmark in horology — awarded only after passing eleven strict criteria of finishing and functional excellence.

03
Hand-Finished Surfaces

Each bridge and plate undergoes weeks of finishing — anglage, perlage, côtes de Genève — invisible to the wearer, irreplaceable in character.
