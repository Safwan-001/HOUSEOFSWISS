Our Heritage

Founded on Principle

House of Swiss was established in 2022 with a singular mission: to create timepieces that honor Swiss watchmaking tradition while pushing the boundaries of horological innovation. Founded by Masnoon Nishad, a visionary with a passion for precision engineering and artistic expression, our maison represents the culmination of decades of expertise and an unwavering commitment to excellence.

We believe that a true timepiece is not merely a tool for measuring time, but a companion for life — a symbol of personal achievement and refined taste. Every watch that bears our name carries this philosophy.

Our Timeline

2022
The Founding
House of Swiss is established in Banani, Dhaka, with a vision to bring Swiss watchmaking excellence to Asia. The atelier is founded with a core team of master craftsmen.
2023
First Collection Launch
Launch of Segment A — Essential Timepieces. The inaugural collection receives recognition from watch enthusiasts and collectors across South Asia.
2023
Geneva Seal Certification
First House of Swiss movements receive the prestigious Poinçon de Genève certification, validating our commitment to Swiss watchmaking standards.
2024
Segment B Debut
Introduction of Segment B — Signature Pieces. These premium watches showcase advanced complications and master-level craftsmanship.
2024
International Recognition
Featured in major watch publications and reviewed by renowned horological experts. Order book extends across Asia, Europe, and Middle East.
2025
Segment C Launch
Unveiling of our Magnus Opus collection — the finest watches ever created by House of Swiss, featuring grand complications and museum-quality craftsmanship.
2025
Atelier Expansion
New atelier space dedicated to complex watchmaking. Additional master craftsmen join our team, enhancing production capacity for custom commissions.
2026
Present Day
House of Swiss stands as the premier luxury watch manufacturer in South Asia, recognized globally for uncompromising quality and innovation.

The Atelier

🏛️

Our Workshop

Nestled in the heart of Banani, Dhaka, our atelier represents a fusion of Swiss precision and South Asian craftsmanship. Spanning over 15,000 square feet, the workspace is meticulously designed to facilitate the creation of the world's finest timepieces.

Our atelier houses state-of-the-art machinery alongside traditional hand-finishing stations. Here, our master watchmakers work in sterile environments, adjusting each movement to within 0.1mm tolerances. The atelier is not merely a factory — it is a temple of horological excellence.

Every visitor to our atelier experiences the passion and dedication that goes into each timepiece. We welcome serious collectors and enthusiasts to witness the creation process firsthand through private tours.

Our Values

Precision

Excellence

Artistry
