After-Sales Service

Maintenance & Servicing
Your House of Swiss timepiece is built to last generations. We recommend professional servicing every 3-5 years. Our master craftsmen perform complete overhauls, ensuring your watch continues to perform at the highest level.
Warranty & Insurance
All House of Swiss watches include a comprehensive 5-year international warranty. We provide documentation for insurance purposes and can assist with insurance claims.
Repairs & Restoration
Whether your watch needs battery replacement, crystal repair, or complete restoration, our craftsmen handle every repair with the same care and precision as a new creation.
Authenticity Verification
We provide detailed certificates of authenticity for all House of Swiss timepieces. Our experts can verify authenticity and condition for collectors and potential buyers.
